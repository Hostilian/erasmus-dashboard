Intra-EU Pet Relocation Guide (Moving with your Cat)

Relocating from the Czech Republic (Prague) with your cat constitutes intra-EU pet travel. Complete the following mandatory steps at least 1 month before your departure date:

  • 1. Microchip Implantation

    Must be ISO 11784/11785 compliant. CRITICAL: The microchip must be implanted *before* or at the exact same time as the rabies vaccination. Vaccinations given before microchipping are invalid for travel.

  • 2. Rabies Vaccination & 21-Day Wait

    The cat must be at least 12 weeks old at the time of vaccination. If it is a primary vaccine, you must wait at least 21 full days before crossing any borders.

  • 3. EU Pet Passport

    Must be issued and completed by a licensed/authorized Czech veterinarian. Keep it up to date with microchip details, rabies vaccination details, and physical description.

Transit Options & Carrier Rules

• Czech Railways (ČD): Very pet-friendly. Your cat travels free of charge if kept in a fully closed, impermeable carrier (max 90x60x40 cm). If booking sleeper/couchette cars (e.g. to Poland or Slovakia), you must purchase the entire compartment.

• Airlines: Budget airlines like Ryanair and Wizz Air have a strict no-pets policy (assistance dogs only). Use full-service carriers (Lufthansa, Austrian Airlines, KLM) to travel with your cat in the cabin (PETC) for a fee of ~€50–€70. The soft-sided carrier must fit under the seat and pet + carrier weight must not exceed 8kg.

Visa & Part-Time Work Rights (Third-Country Nationals)

As a Turkish student with a Czech residence permit, your stay and work rights under the Intra-EU Mobility Directive (EU) 2016/801 vary by country. Below are specific guidelines:

Spain Madrid/Barcelona/Valencia Automatic 30h/week

• Mobility Stay: Allowed up to 360 days. University submits Comunicación de movilidad.
• Work Rights: TCN students are automatically authorized to work part-time up to 30 hours per week (no separate work permit required).
• Action: Employer must register the contract with Spanish Social Security.

Estonia Tallinn/Tartu Unlimited Hours

• Mobility Stay: Allowed up to 360 days. Host university registers stay.
• Work Rights: TCN students have the right to work with no weekly limit on working hours, provided that the employment does not interfere with academic progress.
• Action: Register contract with Estonian Tax Board and get Estonian Tax ID.

Germany Munich/Bonn/Stuttgart 140 Days/Year

• Mobility Stay: Allowed up to 360 days. Host university must notify BAMF 30-40 days before arrival.
• Work Rights: Permitted to work up to 140 full days or 280 half days per year (or 20h/week during semesters). No separate work permit required.
• Action: Ensure host university processes the BAMF notification successfully.

France Lille/Paris/Lyon 964 Hours/Year

• Mobility Stay: Allowed up to 360 days. Host university notifies point de contact within 30 days of arrival.
• Work Rights: Fully exempt from work permits. Permitted to work up to 964 hours per year (~20h/week).
• Action: Employer must notify the Préfecture 48 hours before contract start.

Belgium Brussels/Gembloux Exempt / Holiday Bonus

• Mobility Stay: Allowed up to 360 days. Host university notifies Immigration Office. Requires registration for Annex 33.
• Work Rights: Exempt from work permits. Allowed up to 20 hours per week during terms and full-time (38h/week) during holidays.
• Action: Ensure municipality issues Annex 33 with work permission.

Finland Helsinki/Oulu/Mikkeli 30h/week Average

• Mobility Stay: Allowed up to 360 days. Submit mobility notification to Migri prior to arrival.
• Work Rights: Permitted to work up to 30 hours per week average during academic terms (flexible averaging over the year). No work permit required.
• Action: File Migri mobility notification and pay processing fee in advance.

Portugal Lisbon/Porto/Evora Exempt / 20h/week

• Mobility Stay: Allowed up to 360 days. Must notify AIMA at least 30 days before mobility period.
• Work Rights: Exempt from work permits for up to 20 hours per week during semesters (full-time during breaks).
• Action: Notify AIMA upon beginning employment.

Hungary Budapest Exempt / 30h/week

• Mobility Stay: Allowed up to 360 days. Host university notifies OIF.
• Work Rights: Exempt from work permits. Permitted to work up to 30 hours per week during academic terms (full-time up to 90 days/year in breaks).

Slovenia Ljubljana/Maribor Exempt / 40h/week

Lithuania LTVK Klaipėda Fully Exempt
