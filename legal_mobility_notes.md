## 1. Intra-EU Student Mobility (EU Directive 2016/801)

Since you hold a valid Czech student residence permit, you are protected under **EU Directive 2016/801** for intra-EU mobility. This directive allows you to study in another EU member state (Spain or Portugal) for up to 360 days without applying for a brand-new national student visa from scratch in Prague, provided that you submit a formal mobility notification.

### Spain Procedure (UdL Lleida)
*   **Host Coordinator Contact:** Escola Politècnica Superior (EPS) International Office (`eps.international@udl.cat` | Coordinator: Prof. Álvaro de Gracia).
*   **Action Plan:** The UdL Oficina de Relaciones Internacionales (ORI) must submit a mobility notification (*Autorización de movilidad de alumnos de la Unión Europea*) to the **Delegación del Gobierno en Cataluña**.
*   **Timeline:** This notification must be submitted **at least 1 month before your arrival**. 
*   **Legal Silence:** Under Spanish immigration procedures, if the government does not issue a rejection within **30 days** of filing, the notification is considered administratively approved (*silencio positivo*).
*   **Documents You Must Provide:**
    1.  Full passport scan.
    2.  Valid Czech student residence permit (must be valid for the entire mobility duration).
    3.  Signed Learning Agreement (proving academic continuity).
    4.  Proof of health insurance (European Health Insurance Card - EHIC, or comprehensive private travel insurance).
    5.  Proof of financial means (grant confirmation letter + bank statements proving at least €600/month).

### Portugal Procedure (UPT Porto)
*   **Host Coordinator Contact:** UPT International Relations Office (`ri@upt.pt` / `incomingstudents@upt.pt`).
*   **Action Plan:** UPT must notify the **Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum (AIMA)** of your mobility.
*   **Timeline:** Submit all files immediately upon nomination.
*   **Registration on Arrival:** Within **30 days of entering Portugal**, you must register your presence with the local police or AIMA branch.

## 2. Student Work Rights & Remote Employment

### Spain (UdL Lleida)
*   **Work Hours Limit:** **30 hours per week** (increased from 20h under the reforms in Royal Decree 1155/2024, in force since May 20, 2025).
*   **Automatic Authorization:** As a student in a higher education institution (University degree), your student status automatically includes work authorization. No separate work permit application is required.
*   **Social Security:** Your employer must register your employment contract with the Spanish Social Security system.

### Portugal (UPT Porto)
*   **Work Hours Limit:** **20 hours per week** during the semester; full-time (40h/week) during official university holidays and breaks.
*   **Procedural Requirement:** You must hold your AIMA residence registration card and notify AIMA regarding your professional activity.

### Remote Work Compliance (Working for your Czech Employer)
*   **Tax Residency:** Because your Erasmus stay is for a single semester (less than 183 days), you **do not become a tax resident** in Spain or Portugal. Your tax residency remains in the Czech Republic.
*   **Social Security Coordination (A1 Certificate):** 
    *   If you continue working remotely for your Czech employer while residing in Spain or Portugal, you are active in the host country's territory.
    *   To prevent double social security liabilities or local registration obligations, your Czech employer must apply for a **temporary A1 certificate** from the **Czech Social Security Administration (ČSSZ)**.
    *   This certificate proves that you remain covered by Czech social security legislation, exempting you and your employer from paying contributions in Spain/Portugal.

## 3. Pet Travel Regulations (Prague to Destination)

Relocating your cat requires compliance with non-commercial EU pet movement rules. No quarantine applies if the veterinary checklist is met.

### Veterinary Checklist
1.  **ISO Microchip:** Compliant with ISO 11784/11785. *Must be implanted before the rabies vaccine is administered.*
2.  **Rabies Vaccination:** Must be active and given at least **21 days prior to departure**.
3.  **EU Pet Passport:** Issued by any authorized veterinarian in Prague, showing the microchip ID and up-to-date vaccine records.
4.  *No tapeworm treatment (Echinococcus) is required for cats entering Spain or Portugal.*

## 4. Cyprus (Nicosia) - Ruled Out & Red-Flagged

The University of Cyprus (CY NICOSIA 01) is a red-flagged option for the following critical reasons:
*   **Geopolitical & Visa Barrier:** Although Cyprus transposes EU Directive 2016/801, it is not a Schengen Member State. While it generally allows holders of Schengen residence permits (like your Czech Student Residence Permit) visa-free entry, **Turkish passport holders are explicitly excluded** from this exemption. You would be required to apply for a Cypriot national entry visa, which is extremely difficult to obtain in practice due to the lack of diplomatic relations between the Republic of Cyprus and Turkey.
*   **Academic Deadline:** The application deadline for the fall semester was **May 1st**, which has already passed.
*   **Language & Course Match:** Instruction for BSc Informatics is primarily in Greek (only informal English support/materials are offered), and UCY requires a minimum GPA of 6.5/10.
*   **Housing & Climbing Scene:** Renting in Nicosia is moderately expensive (€270–€490/mo for a shared room, €530–€650/mo for a studio). There are active climbing/bouldering gyms (e.g., *Ungravity*, *Rockstar*), but the visa barrier makes UCY an absolute no-go.
