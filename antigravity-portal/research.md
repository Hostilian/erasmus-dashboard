## 1. Classical General Relativity & Gravitational Curvature

In Albert Einstein’s **General Relativity (1915)**, gravity is not defined as an attractive vector force acting at a distance, but as the manifestation of **spacetime geometry**. 

### The Einstein Field Equations (EFE)
$$G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}$$

*   $G_{\mu\nu}$ represents the Einstein Tensor, describing the local curvature of spacetime.
*   $g_{\mu\nu}$ is the metric tensor.
*   $\Lambda$ is the cosmological constant.
*   $T_{\mu\nu}$ is the Stress-Energy Tensor, describing the density and flux of energy, momentum, shear stress, and pressure.

### Constraints on Repulsion (Antigravity)
The EFE dictate that spacetime curvature is directly coupled to mass-energy. Since all observed and validated forms of matter and energy have positive values:
1.  **Gravity is always attractive:** Spacetime curves inward toward mass-energy concentrations.
2.  **No Gravitational Shielding:** Unlike electromagnetism (which has positive and negative charges that can screen fields), mass has only one sign. Spacetime curvature cannot be blocked, absorbed, or shielded.

## 2. The Search for Quantum Gravity (QG)

Quantum Gravity seeks to unify General Relativity with the Standard Model of particle physics. Currently, the two theories are incompatible: General Relativity treats spacetime as a smooth, continuous manifold, whereas Quantum Mechanics requires discrete field quantization.

### Candidate Theories
*   **String Theory / M-Theory:** Proposes that fundamental entities are vibrating strings. The graviton emerges as a massless spin-2 excitation of closed strings.
*   **Loop Quantum Gravity (LQG):** Quantizes spacetime directly, predicting discrete nodes of volume and area (spin networks) at the Planck scale.
*   **Emergent Gravity (Entropic Gravity):** Proposes that gravity is not a fundamental interaction but an entropic force arising from statistical mechanics of quantum information.

## 3. Theoretical Warp Mechanics (The Alcubierre Metric)

In 1994, physicist Miguel Alcubierre proposed a spacetime metric that mathematically allows faster-than-light (FTL) transit relative to external frames.

### Mechanics of the Warp Bubble
The metric contracts spacetime in front of a ship and expands it behind:
*   The vessel resides in a "bubble" of flat spacetime.
*   The bubble itself moves through space, avoiding local violations of Special Relativity (the speed of light remains invariant locally).

### Severe Physical Constraints
1.  **Exotic Matter:** The expansion of spacetime behind the bubble requires negative energy density ($T_{00} < 0$), violating the **Weak Energy Condition (WEC)**. Macroscopic negative mass has never been observed.
2.  **Horizon Problem:** The front boundary of the warp bubble is causally disconnected from the spacecraft's interior. A crew cannot transmit signals to control or halt the bubble.
3.  **Destructive Hawking Radiation:** Semiclassical analysis shows that quantum fields would undergo intense particle creation, accumulating Hawking radiation at the bubble wall, incinerating the ship.

## 4. Debunking Pseudoscientific Claims

Several historical "antigravity" experiments have been thoroughly debunked as electromagnetic or aerodynamic forces.

### The Biefeld-Brown Effect & Ionocrafts (Lifters)
*   **The Claim:** Thomas Townsend Brown claimed that asymmetric capacitors subjected to extremely high DC voltage (30kV+) generate a thrust linking electricity directly to gravity (electrogravitics).
*   **The Reality:** The propulsion is entirely **electrohydrodynamic (EHD)**. The high voltage ionizes air molecules at the thin emitter wire. The ions are accelerated toward the wide collector plate by the electric field, colliding with neutral air molecules to create a downward "ion wind" (thrust).
*   **The Proof:** Lifters do not produce any thrust when placed inside a hard vacuum chamber, proving they require air molecules as reaction mass.

### Magnetic Levitation
*   **The Claim:** Superconductors and Maglev trains demonstrate gravity cancellation.
*   **The Reality:** These are purely electromagnetic forces (Meissner effect or electromagnetic induction) opposing the gravitational vector force. The local spacetime curvature and gravitational field remain completely unchanged.
