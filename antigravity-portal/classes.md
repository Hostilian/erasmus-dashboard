# Academic Class Selection - Gravity & Propulsion Physics

Recommended academic pathway for students targeting research in spacetime metrics, quantum gravity, and advanced aerospace propulsion.

---

## Winter Semester Recommended Schedule

To build the mathematical and physical foundations necessary to comprehend General Relativity and advanced propulsion systems, a student should enroll in the following courses:

| Course Code | Course Title | Credits | Prerequisite Requirements | Core Topic Areas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PHYS 4010** | Mathematical Methods for Physicists | 4 | Multivariable Calculus, Linear Algebra | Tensor calculus, complex integration, differential equations |
| **PHYS 4100** | Advanced Electromagnetism | 4 | Intro EM, Differential Equations | Maxwell's equations, relativistic field tensors, wave propagation |
| **PHYS 4200** | Quantum Mechanics I | 4 | Linear Algebra, Classical Mechanics | Hilbert spaces, spin operators, perturbation theory |
| **AERO 4300** | Fluid Dynamics & Aerodynamics | 3 | Thermodynamics, Vector Calculus | Navier-Stokes equations, compressible flow, boundary layers |
| **PHYS 5010** | General Relativity & Gravitation | 4 | PHYS 4010, Classical Mechanics | Differential geometry, Einstein field equations, Schwarzschild metric |
| **AERO 5420** | Advanced Aerospace Propulsion | 3 | AERO 4300, Thermodynamics | Rocket dynamics, electrostatic/ion thrusters, warp metrics |

**Total Semester Load:** 22 Credits

## Course Syllabi & Relevance to Gravity Research

### 1. PHYS 5010: General Relativity & Gravitation
*   **Description:** A rigorous derivation of Einstein's gravity, applying it to black holes, cosmology, and gravitational waves.
*   **Textbooks:** 
    *   *Spacetime and Geometry: An Introduction to General Relativity* by Sean Carroll.
    *   *Gravity: An Introduction to Einstein's General Relativity* by James B. Hartle.
*   **Gravity Relevance:** This is the primary theoretical class. It teaches students how to solve for spacetime curvature, evaluate energy conditions, and mathematically analyze the Alcubierre warp drive metric.

### 2. PHYS 4100: Advanced Electromagnetism
*   **Description:** Maxwell's formulation of electrodynamics and relativistic field transformations.
*   **Textbooks:** 
    *   *Classical Electrodynamics* by John David Jackson.
*   **Gravity Relevance:** Introduces covariant classical field theory and the electromagnetic field tensor $F^{\mu\nu}$, providing a template for how general relativity treats physical fields. Also essential for debunking electrogravitics (Biefeld-Brown) by understanding electric field charge acceleration.

### 3. PHYS 4200: Quantum Mechanics I
*   **Description:** Wave functions, operators, and perturbations in Hilbert spaces.
*   **Textbooks:** 
    *   *Introduction to Quantum Mechanics* by David J. Griffiths.
*   **Gravity Relevance:** Required to understand Casimir effect (microscopic negative energy densities) and the quantization of fields, laying the groundwork for studying String Theory and Loop Quantum Gravity.

### 4. AERO 5420: Advanced Aerospace Propulsion
*   **Description:** Air-breathing, chemical rocket, and electric propulsion technologies.
*   **Textbooks:**
    *   *Mechanics and Thermodynamics of Propulsion* by Hill & Peterson.
*   **Gravity Relevance:** Covers ion and electrostatic thrusters (which are often conflated with antigravity lifters) and teaches the engineering baseline of rocket propulsion to contrast against hypothetical spacetime-bending transit.
